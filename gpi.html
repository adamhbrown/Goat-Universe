Real rankings.

To establish who is #1, #2 or #10 on a block, eligible participants need a privately verified location, a consistent GOAT Block identifier, and approved GPI scores. Nearby phones or a shared ZIP code are not sufficient.

01 / VerifyA member opts in and privately verifies an eligible address. No public street addresses.
02 / MatchA secure mapping service assigns a common street-segment ID between intersections, with a reviewed fallback for unusual streets or ambiguous boundaries. This is not necessarily a Census block.
03 / RankOnly verified participating members with eligible official results appear. A rank never claims to represent neighbors who have not joined.
