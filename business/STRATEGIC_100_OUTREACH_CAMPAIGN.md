# GOAT Universe — 100 Strategic Relationships Campaign
Date: 2026-10-17 | Status: research and preparation; no outreach authorized or sent.

## Founder positioning
GOAT Universe is a national sports competition platform and entertainment ecosystem. Its five GOAT Games are the core participation and revenue products. GOATville is the cinematic destination, not the business model. Commissioner of Sports demonstrates the creative ambition: make sports larger than life. Do not pitch local graphics, posters or a local-first agency.

## First 100 targets: allocation, not fabricated contacts
- 35 sports-tech / consumer-tech / gaming / media venture investors and growth investors
- 20 sports executives, league operators, and distribution leaders
- 15 athlete, creator and sports marketing agencies
- 15 media, streaming, production and sports entertainment companies
- 15 strategic technology, data, sponsorship and commerce partners

These are **target slots**, not researched people. No invented emails, investor interest, or warm introductions. Build verified records from public/permissioned sources.

## Target record schema
target_id; organization; person; role; category; investment/partnership thesis; evidence URL; source date; public business contact or verified introduction route; warm intro path; priority; founder-approved outreach status; last action; next action; reply; opt-out/suppression; notes; owner.

## Scoring
Score fit 0–5, sector relevance 0–5, ability to add distribution 0–5, stage/check-size compatibility 0–5 (investors only), credible introduction route 0–5. Show source evidence and confidence. Never substitute prestige for actual fit.

## Three tailored narratives
Investor: 20-year competition IP; five participation products; monetization hypotheses and unit economics; functional demo and validation roadmap; clear use of funds. Do not promise returns or assert unverified traction.
Executive / distribution: recurring fan participation, branded leagues, championships, audience engagement, measurable pilots.
Agency / media: larger-than-life competition storytelling, rights-safe branded content, creator/athlete activations and sponsorship integrations.

## Materials to build before outreach
1. Founder-approved 12-slide investor deck with product screenshots and accurate build status.
2. 90-second live demo: cinematic entrance -> games -> participant journey -> standings -> media layer.
3. One-page opportunity overview, defensibility/IP summary and explicit financial scenario assumptions.
4. Data room index: incorporation, ownership/IP, privacy/security, cap table, financial model, product roadmap and pilot evidence. Mark unavailable items clearly.
5. Three personalized message templates and one follow-up each; reviewed before use.
6. CRM with verified sources, no duplicate contacts, opt-out precedence, human approval, audit trail and follow-up reminders.

## Execution gates
Research first; then shortlist 100 verified, relevant targets; then founder review of messaging and claims; then staged outreach in small measured cohorts through approved channels. Respect anti-spam rules, platform terms, privacy, and recipient opt-outs. Never auto-send to an unverified or suppressed recipient. Track reply, meeting, partnership, and investment-process milestones without fabricating outcomes.
