## Strategy: one company, connected lines of business

GOATVERSE HOLDINGS INC. is the parent brand framework. GOAT Universe is the primary customer platform; GOATVILLE is its digital destination and developing lifestyle merchandise identity. The five GOAT Games are the core participation products, not a mere teaser for merchandise.

| Track | First customer action | Proposed revenue source | Prerequisite for live sales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GOAT Games | Discover an official/hosted league and account | Approved contest fees, platform portion | Rules, eligibility, compliant payments, hosted accounts |
| GOATVILLE apparel | Explore premium collections | Direct-to-consumer margin; selective wholesale | Source artwork clearance, suppliers, landed COGS, tax/returns, checkout |
| GST & named marks | Recognize the brand, inquire about use | Product collaborations and licensing royalties | Clearance and executed license agreements |
| GOATVERSE Creative Media | Receive league-specific editorial | Sponsorship/advertising/licensing opportunities | Editorial automation QA, rights review, sponsor approval |
| GOATVILLE residency | Become eligible through the five Games | Increased retention / ancillary commerce | Account and entitlement source of truth |
| Strategic partnerships | Contact founder | Sponsorships, distribution or equity on approved terms | Founder-reviewed proposals and legal review |

Every line needs separately measured unit economics. Shared accounts, content and brand recognition do not justify conflating fees, inventory liabilities, member balances, royalties or accounting.

## Product lifecycle, before checkout

`CONCEPT → ARTWORK REVIEW → RIGHTS REVIEW → SUPPLIER QUOTE/SAMPLE → ECONOMIC REVIEW → FOUNDER APPROVED → PUBLIC LISTING → ORDER ENABLED`

- Each real sellable SKU requires a unique ID, approved images, material/size/variant, supplier, per-unit landed costs, lead time, fulfillment rules, return/refund policy, taxes, stock policy, customer support owner, and launch approval.
- Before ordering samples, compare embroidery/print methods, minimum quantities and fulfillment models. Premium finishes have to survive real quality control, not just rendered imagery.
- Published product prices and availability must originate from authorized inventory data, never from concept copy.
- Checkout is a separate integration gate. No credit card collection or pretend cart action in founder preview.
- Commerce customer identities may integrate with existing member accounts only after hosted Auth, data handling, privacy, refunds and transaction separation are verified.
- For a proposed licensing deal, track mark/artwork scope, approval rights, territory, channel, exclusivity, minimum guarantees, royalty calculation, audit rights and termination/reversion.

## GST invariants

- Official GST wall clocks use IANA timezone `America/New_York` with the same instant as New York City.
- Both displayed clocks are derived from the same Date instance and formatter; update at least once per second, including automatic winter/summer DST transitions.
- GST is branded cultural terminology, **not** a separate UTC offset or an alternative civil/legal timezone.

## Integration and reporting

Maintain one nightly integration line, one connected founder walkthrough and a verifiable SHA. One consolidated private preview is the objective; GitHub branches alone are **not** hosted preview deployments.

All active development uses separate review branches with PR checks. No direct merge to main, public promotion, product sales, outbound partner commitments, migrations or payments until founder acceptance and release sign-off.

Report separately each week: official game entrants and gross/net fees (when live); acquisition and retained members; apparel conversion, net sales, refunds and gross margin; licensing pipeline, signed agreements and recognized royalties; media reach/sponsor yield; operating burn and cash runway. Never present target figures as actual revenue.
